What a P6 Schedule Needs From the Tool That Opens It

Reads XER and PMXML Directly

Primavera P6's XER and PMXML exchange formats open in the browser — no P6 licence, no database to attach, no Citrix session. The same import also reads Microsoft Project .MPP, MPT, MPX, and XML, which is what most mixed-contractor projects actually need.

Real CPM, Not a Timeline Strip

Critical Path Method with 4 relationship types (FS, SS, FF, SF), 8 constraint types, elapsed durations, custom calendars and work weeks, and multiple critical paths. Move an activity and the downstream work and the float recalculate.

Resource Leveling and Rate Tables

Work, material, cost, budget, and generic resources. Automatic leveling by priority, slack, or ID, leveling within available slack, rate tables A–E with date-effective rates, time-varying availability, overtime rates, and cost per use.

Baselines and Earned Value

11 baselines (0–10), 10 interim plans, 55 baseline columns plus 5 variance columns, progress lines, and 13 Earned Value columns — BCWS, BCWP, CPI, SPI, EAC, VAC, TCPI — on % Complete or Physical % Complete.

Built for the Schedules Construction Actually Runs

Long activity lists, custom work weeks and non-working days, levelled crews, cost loading, and progress against a baseline. The domain vocabulary is the domain's own, because softening it costs credibility with a planner.

Anyone on the Project Can Open It

Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge, on Mac, Windows, Linux, or a Chromebook. A site engineer, a client-side PM, or a commercial manager can read the programme without a seat on the scheduling software.

Where Primavera P6 wins, and where this is the better tool

These are not the same product competing on price. P6 is enterprise portfolio management; Ingantt is the schedule. The useful question is which of the two jobs you are actually doing.

Primavera P6 wins on enterprise portfolio management, and it is not close. A multi-project enterprise project structure, a shared enterprise resource pool across programmes, timesheet capture, organisation-wide activity coding standards, portfolio-level reporting and governance — that is what P6 is for, and it is why large contractors and owner organisations standardise on it. If your work is running a programme of many interlinked projects under one governance model, Ingantt is not a replacement and this page is not going to pretend otherwise.

Ingantt wins when the job is the schedule itself — and specifically when the people who need to read or re-plan it are not the people with the P6 seats. A subcontractor checking whether the programme they have been handed is achievable. A client-side PM reviewing a contractor's monthly update. A commercial manager tracing which activities actually drive the completion date. All of them need real CPM, real float, real baselines, and the file to open on whatever laptop they have. None of them need an enterprise project structure.

The export direction is worth stating plainly before anyone commits. Ingantt opens XER and PMXML, but it exports MS Project XML, PDF, PNG, CSV, YAML, and Markdown — it does not write XER or PMXML back out. So a P6 schedule can come here to be read, analysed, and re-planned, and the result can go to someone on Microsoft Project as XML or to a stakeholder as a PDF. What it cannot do is hand a revised programme back into P6 in P6's own format.
