Two products, two purchase models

This is the single thing most worth getting straight before paying for anything, because a subscription and a one-time app purchase are not interchangeable and buying the wrong one is a refund request.

Ingantt for Web is a subscription. It runs at web.ingantt.com in any browser, saves to your Google Drive, and is billed per user — $7.99 per user per month, or $71.88 per user per year billed annually, which works out at $5.99 per user per month. It starts with a 7-day trial that unlocks every feature and needs no credit card. Web is also the current build, so it is where the feature set described across this site applies in full.

The platform apps are one-time paid purchases, sold per platform. Ingantt for Android, iOS, Windows, and macOS are bought once in Google Play, the App Store, the Mac App Store, and the Microsoft Store respectively, at the price shown on that listing. They are separate purchases from each other and from the web subscription: buying the Android app does not give you the Windows app, and it does not give you Ingantt for Web.

Two consequences are worth stating rather than leaving to be discovered. The 7-day trial and the subscription prices on this page apply to Ingantt for Web only — they are not a trial of the store apps. And the Android app and the web app are the current builds, while Windows, macOS, and iOS are older builds with their own older behaviour and their own documentation, so a feature that exists on the web is not automatically in the app a store visitor is about to buy. Check the platform page for the one you are buying.
